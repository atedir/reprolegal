What happens next

  1. We read what you sent and check which destinations your nationality allows.
  2. You receive a written reply with the itemised figures for the programmes that actually apply to you.
  3. If it looks workable, we book a 30-minute call — no deck, no pressure.
While you wait
Two things worth reading before the call: the Länder-Finder, which shows what your passport allows, and the cost breakdown, so the numbers on the call are not a surprise.
