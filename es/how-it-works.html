Inicio · How it works

Cinco etapas, cada una definida antes de empezar

Los plazos son rangos habituales. Cuando algo depende de la biología, lo decimos en lugar de prometer una fecha.

01

Requisitos y documentos

We check what your country of residence requires, then prepare and apostille your documents against a written checklist before anything is submitted. 15% of the programme falls due here.

1–4 semanas
02

FIV y embriones

Un ciclo completo en una clínica autorizada, o la transferencia de sus embriones congelados. Test genético cuando esté indicado médicamente.

4–8 semanas
03

Asignación de la gestante

You receive a full profile — medical history, psychological evaluation, home visit notes — and meet her on a call before any medical step. You can decline a match. 25% falls due once you accept.

2–6 semanas
04

Embarazo y seguimiento

Scheduled check-ups, regular updates, direct contact with your surrogate if both sides want it, and a coordinator answering within 24 hours. 25% at transfer, 20% at heartbeat.

~9 meses
05

Nacimiento, registro y salida del país

Birth certificate, translations and the full document package for your embassy. Exit steps differ by nationality, and we walk each one with you. Final 15% on handover.

2–8 semanas tras el nacimiento

What you pay, and when

Five tranches — 15 / 25 / 25 / 20 / 15 — each tied to a completed and documented stage. We do not hold your funds between stages and we never ask for the programme upfront.

Where it can go wrong

Three things account for most delays: a document rejected at apostille, a cycle that does not produce a viable embryo, and a court calendar in destinations that use a post-birth procedure. The first we control, the second nobody does, the third we plan around by choosing the destination carefully.

¿Listos para empezar?

Una llamada de 30 minutos, sin presentación y sin presión. Si su país complica esta vía, lo sabrán primero.
