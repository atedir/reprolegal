Cost by destination

The same programme costs very differently depending on where it runs. Ranges by destination are on each country page; the widest gap on our list is between the Caucasus and the United States, and it is roughly threefold.

What is never included

  • Legal steps in your own country after birth
  • Your travel and accommodation
  • Additional transfer cycles beyond those contracted
  • Neonatal care beyond routine, if it is needed

We name these upfront because they are the four items that turn a quoted price into a surprise.
