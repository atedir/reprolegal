Start from where you actually are

The right programme depends on your embryos, your medical history and your passport — not on which package sells best.

Frozen embryo transfer

Your existing embryos are shipped, matched to a screened surrogate and transferred — no retrieval cycle needed. The fastest route to transfer and the cheapest, because the IVF stage is already behind you.

Own eggs and surrogacy

A full IVF cycle with the intended mother's eggs, genetic testing where indicated, then transfer to a matched surrogate. Priced per cycle, so you always know what a second attempt costs before you need one.

Egg donation and surrogacy

We do not run a catalogue and do not resell donor profiles. A donor is sourced individually against your criteria and fully screened before you are asked to decide anything. The cycle is never shared with another recipient.

Single fathers and mothers

Jurisdiction decides most of this — several destinations restrict single applicants outright. We start with what your passport allows and work backwards.

Same-sex couples

Programmes where both partners are recognised, and a documented route to registering your child at home. Recognition is mapped before you commit, not after.

HIV, hepatitis, prior failed cycles

Protocol-specific clinics, sperm washing where indicated, and an honest review of previous attempts. No blanket promises — if the odds are poor, you will hear the number.

Which of these applies also depends on where the programme runs. Check both together in the country matcher.

A 30-minute call, no deck and no pressure. If your country makes this path hard, you will hear it first.
