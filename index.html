What we commit to

What you can hold us to

Fixed core pricing

Coordination, surrogate compensation and medical management are fixed in the contract. Variable items are named, capped where possible, and never invoiced as a surprise.

Payment by milestone

One coordinator

The same person from your first call through birth and exit. You never re-explain your history to someone new.

Independent counsel

You get your own lawyer, not ours. Contracts are reviewed by someone whose duty is to you.

Questions

Before you start

How much does a programme cost in total?
It depends on the destination, on whether you already have embryos, and on whether a donor is needed. The explorer above shows the typical split; your itemised quote follows a short call about your medical and legal starting point.
When do I pay, and how much at a time?
In five tranches tied to milestones: signing, surrogate matched, transfer completed, heartbeat confirmed, birth and documents. Nothing is paid for a stage that has not happened.
How long does the whole process take?
Typically 14 to 20 months from first documents to flying home, though matching and IVF response are biological variables.
Is success guaranteed?
No. Pregnancy and birth depend on medical factors nobody can guarantee. What is contractually defined is what happens — clinically and financially — if a cycle does not work.
Will my country recognise my child?
The answer is specific to your nationality. Some countries recognise the foreign birth certificate; others require a court order or an adoption step at home. We map your route with local counsel before you commit.
