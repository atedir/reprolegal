Surrogacy in Czechia

No dedicated surrogacy statute. The route runs through established medical practice and family-law procedure, which means the legal sequence matters more here than anywhere else on this list.

Eligibility here is only half the question. The other half is your own nationality: some countries recognise a foreign birth certificate, others require a court order or an adoption step once you are home. Use the country matcher to see both tests together.

How a programme in Czechia runs

The five stages are the same everywhere we work — eligibility and documents, IVF and embryos, surrogate matching, pregnancy and monitoring, then birth, registration and exit. What changes by destination is the paperwork sequence and how long you spend in country after birth. The full sequence is set out on how it works.

What it costs

A programme in Czechia typically falls in the $55k – $70k band, with the split between surrogate compensation, coordination, medical and legal shown item by item on the costs page. Payment is in five tranches tied to milestones — nothing is paid for a stage that has not happened.

What we will tell you honestly

If your nationality makes Czechia a poor fit, we will say so on the first call and point you at the destinations that work instead. That is cheaper for everyone than discovering it after the contracts are signed.

Verification
Every legal statement on this page is confirmed with local counsel before publication and re-checked for your specific nationality when we quote you. Surrogacy law in this region has changed more than once in the last five years.
